# Dragged neutral bar + stronger detents

- Horizontal neutral movement is continuous and visibly dragged, like vertical movement.
- Removed the teleport/checkpoint-style horizontal jumps.
- Left, center, and right neutral positions remain as soft detents at the 1/2, 3/4, and 5/6 columns.
- Near a detent, horizontal travel slows down and small movement settles the knob into the neutral position.
- Continued deliberate sideways movement can push through the detent toward the next position.
- Vertical gear entry is only allowed when the knob is close to one of those three detents.
- Forgiving diagonal side-entry from a gear into Neutral remains.
- Reverse / 1st toggle remains unchanged.

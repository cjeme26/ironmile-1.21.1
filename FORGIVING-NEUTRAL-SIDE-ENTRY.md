# Forgiving neutral side-entry

- Keeps the three neutral alignment slots.
- Neutral slot changes need slightly less horizontal mouse travel.
- When pulling out of a gear, the stick no longer has to hit the exact center line before sideways movement can help.
- Once the stick is reasonably close to neutral, a clear sideways/diagonal gesture lets it fall into neutral early.
- The horizontal part of that same gesture is preserved, so shifts such as 3 -> 5 can flow naturally into the next neutral slot.
- Sideways wobble is still ignored while the stick is deep inside a gear.
- Reverse/1st toggle is unchanged.

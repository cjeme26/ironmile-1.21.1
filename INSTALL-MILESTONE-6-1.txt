IRON MILE — MILESTONE 6.1 DRIVETRAIN TUNING

Do not commit the original milestone-six tuning first. This update replaces it
with the revised values based on the first driving test.

1. Quit the development Minecraft client.
2. Extract this ZIP into Downloads.
3. In VS Code, make sure the terminal is at the root of ironmile-1.21.1.
4. Run:

      ditto "$HOME/Downloads/milestone6-1-update" .
      ./gradlew clean runClient

CHANGES

- Peak torque: 300 Nm -> 450 Nm
- Closer six-speed ratios and shorter final drive
- Expected full-throttle shifts: approximately 49, 78 and 113 km/h
- Expected dry/summer-tire 0–100: approximately 13 seconds
- High-speed braking unchanged
- Braking assistance progressively increases below 35 km/h
- Near-stop braking assistance is up to 75% stronger

TESTS

- Time 0–100 approximately using the Minecraft day-time counter or a stopwatch.
- Check whether acceleration still feels heavy but no longer frustrating.
- Hold S around 10 mph / 16 km/h; stopping should now be quick.
- Confirm high-speed braking still feels similar to the first version.
- Release W around 80 km/h and observe coasting: report whether the car slows
  naturally, too quickly, or for too long.
- Confirm the shifts show N briefly and RPM falls after each upshift.
- Confirm tires, surfaces and weather still affect available drivetrain force.

After successful tuning, commit with:
  Add and tune six-speed automatic drivetrain

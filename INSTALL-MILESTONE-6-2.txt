IRON MILE — MILESTONE 6.2 EVERYDAY-CAR DRIVETRAIN

This update can be installed over milestone 6.0 or 6.1. Existing Git commits do
not need to be removed or rewritten.

1. Quit the development Minecraft client.
2. Extract this ZIP into Downloads.
3. In VS Code, make sure the terminal is at the root of ironmile-1.21.1.
4. Run:

      ditto "$HOME/Downloads/milestone6-2-update" .
      ./gradlew clean runClient

PHYSICS CORRECTION

Wheel force now equals wheel torque divided by wheel radius. The missing
division made the earlier 450 Nm engine accelerate much too slowly. With the
correction, the car uses more believable everyday specifications:

- Peak engine torque: 210 Nm
- Vehicle mass: 1,500 kg
- Expected dry/summer-tire 0–100: approximately 9.5 seconds
- Expected full-throttle shifts: approximately 49, 78 and 113 km/h
- Expected maximum speed: approximately 140 km/h

BRAKING

- Base braking is approximately 17% stronger at every speed.
- Assistance increases below 35 km/h.
- Near-stop braking receives up to a 90% boost.

TESTS

- Test acceleration on a dry road with summer tires.
- Confirm it feels like a normal modern automatic, not an old car or supercar.
- Compare braking near 100, 50 and 16 km/h.
- Confirm low-speed stops are quick without snapping unnaturally.
- Release W around 80 km/h to test coasting.

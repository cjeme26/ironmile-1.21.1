CORRECTION

The vehicle previously used exactly zero vertical velocity while grounded.
Minecraft could therefore alternate the car between grounded and airborne
states. Because drivetrain force is only applied with tire contact, this could
roughly halve the number of powered ticks.

A small downward grounding force now keeps collision contact active on every
flat-road tick. It does not increase engine torque or alter tire grip.

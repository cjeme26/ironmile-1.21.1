IRON MILE — MILESTONE 6 AUTOMATIC DRIVETRAIN

Before installing, commit the working milestone-five build in VS Code with:
  Add wet-weather grip

Then:

1. Quit the development Minecraft client.
2. Extract this ZIP into Downloads.
3. In VS Code, make sure the terminal is at the root of ironmile-1.21.1.
4. Run:

      ditto "$HOME/Downloads/milestone6-update" .
      ./gradlew clean runClient

FIRST TEST: DRY ROAD WITH SUMMER TIRES

- Hold W continuously. Watch RPM rise, N appear briefly during shifts, and RPM
  fall after each upshift.
- Expected full-throttle shifts are approximately 57, 96 and 139 km/h.
- Expected maximum speed is approximately 140 km/h.
- Release W at high speed. The transmission should select economy gears D5/D6
  and the car should slow through rolling resistance, engine braking and drag.
- Press W again after slowing in a high gear. The gearbox should downshift when
  RPM is too low (kickdown).
- Hold S while moving forward: it must brake first, then show R and reverse only
  after nearly stopping.

SECOND TEST: EXISTING PHYSICS

- Confirm summer/all-season/winter tires still install and persist.
- Confirm dry, wet, sheltered and mixed weather conditions still work.
- Confirm snow and ice still limit drivetrain force and make the car slide.

REPORT THESE FEELINGS BEFORE TUNING

- Is 0–100 km/h too slow, too fast, or suitable for a heavy road car?
- Are shifts too frequent, too late, or too abrupt?
- Does releasing W slow the car too quickly?
- Does braking feel too strong or weak?
- Does the car remain stable near 140 km/h?

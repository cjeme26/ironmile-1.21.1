IronMile dismount nudge fix

This patch removes the tiny immediate client rewind that could happen when
leaving a moving car. Small (< 0.18 block / 4 degree) differences between the
last server snapshot and the locally predicted position are ignored at the
exact handoff moment. Larger differences still reconcile normally.

Apply from the IronMile project root without opening the ZIP:
  ditto -x -k "$HOME/Downloads/IronMile-dismount-nudge-fix.zip" .

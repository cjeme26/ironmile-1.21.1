IronMile high-speed exit braking fix

Root cause fixed:
- The exit-braking flag was set after dismounting, but the braking calculation
  was inside applyThrottleAndBrakes(), which only runs while a driver is seated.
- The unoccupied car therefore used ordinary rolling resistance instead.

New behavior:
- At or below 10 km/h: stop immediately.
- Above 10 km/h: remove about 22% of remaining forward speed each tick, with a
  minimum braking force, then snap to rest at 10 km/h.
- Even close to maximum speed, the car should stop in roughly half a second.

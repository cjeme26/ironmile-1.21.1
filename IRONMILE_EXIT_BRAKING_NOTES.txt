IronMile exit-braking follow-up patch

Behavior:
- At or below 10 km/h, exiting stops the car immediately.
- Above 10 km/h, a strong speed-scaled parking brake slows it quickly.
- Entering the car cancels the automatic exit brake.

Apply from the project root:
  ditto -x -k "$HOME/Downloads/IronMile-exit-braking-patch.zip" .
  ./gradlew clean runClient

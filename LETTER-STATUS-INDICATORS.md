# Letter status indicators

Replaces the dashboard-symbol experiment with three simple Minecraft-font letters.

Bottom-left:
- H = headlights
- F = low fuel
- E = engine/stall warning

Inactive letters are dark grey.
- H lights green when headlights are on.
- F lights amber at <=15% fuel.
- E lights red when stalled or out of fuel.

No icon textures, no background panel, no other HUD/layout changes.

# Lighter shifter + driving left-click lock

- Reduced shifter pushback substantially.
- Increased direct mouse response so moving between gears is easier.
- Weakened the magnetic rail pull and neutral centering.
- Kept the forgiving snap zones and a small final gear detent.
- Left click can no longer attack or break blocks while controlling either an automatic or manual IronMile car.
- Left click behaves normally again as soon as the player leaves the car.
- Right-click manual shifting and R/F fallback controls are unchanged.

Iron Mile creative-tab repair patch

This patch adds the missing ModItemGroups.java file and registers the custom
Iron Mile creative inventory tab during common mod initialization.

The tab uses the Yellow Hatchback item as its icon and contains:
- Yellow Hatchback
- Summer Tires
- All-Season Tires
- Winter Tires

It also removes the same items from Minecraft's generic Tools tab so they are
not duplicated.

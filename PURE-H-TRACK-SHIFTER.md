# Pure H-track shifter

- Removed automatic centering, magnetic pull, resistance, and wall-bounce logic.
- The knob now follows only the visible H geometry.
- In a gear lane it can only move vertically.
- Once it reaches the middle Neutral bar it can move left/right.
- Near the 1/2, 3/4, or 5/6 columns, vertical movement becomes available.
- Fast mouse movement is split into small legal steps so it cannot skip over the track.
- Left click now TOGGLES the push-down reverse lockout while right click holds the shifter.
  Press once: upper-left is Reverse.
  Press again: upper-left is 1st.

# Slower neutral horizontal movement

- Keeps the three neutral checkpoints.
- Keeps one-neutral-slot-per-mouse-sample protection.
- Keeps forgiving diagonal side-entry into Neutral.
- Vertical shifter sensitivity stays at 0.012.
- Horizontal sensitivity is reduced from 0.012 to 0.0065 (about 46% slower).
- Removed the previous 1.15x horizontal boost during side-entry so diagonal exits do not suddenly jump across neutral slots.
- Reverse / 1st toggle is unchanged.

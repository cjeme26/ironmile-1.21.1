# Steering response tune

This is a focused handling adjustment for the current hatchback.

What changed
- Preserves the existing speed-sensitive steering curve.
- Adds a smooth steering-authority boost that fades as road speed rises:
  - roughly +28% at very low / parking speed
  - roughly +15-22% through ordinary road speeds
  - roughly +8% at maximum speed
- Reverse steering direction, tire/surface grip influence, lateral grip, drivetrain,
  braking, and maximum speeds are unchanged.

Goal
Make tight manoeuvres and normal 90-degree intersection turns easier without
making the car twitchy or unsafe at high speed.

IRON MILE — MILESTONE 10.0.1 RENDER CORRECTION

Install from the Iron Mile project folder:

  ditto "$HOME/Downloads/milestone10-0-1-update" .
  ./gradlew clean runClient

This is a small overlay for Milestone 10. Keep the Milestone 10 model and
texture files already installed in your project.

What changed:

- Corrected OBJ triangles for Minecraft's four-vertex render layer.
- Removed the long black/yellow stretched shards caused by malformed faces.
- Reduced and repositioned the temporary headlight, brake-light, and
  reversing-light overlays so they sit closer to the real lamp clusters.

Please check before committing:

1. Spawn a fresh car and walk around every side.
2. Confirm there are no stretched spikes, shards, or faces crossing the car.
3. Confirm the yellow body texture and windows look coherent.
4. Drive forward and reverse; confirm all four wheels rotate cleanly.
5. Steer left and right; confirm the front wheels visibly steer.
6. Press H and test headlights, braking lights, and reversing lights.
7. Mount and dismount, then check the player position.
8. Drive close to a full-block wall and report whether the visible car passes
   too far into it. Visual length-aware collision will be handled separately
   if necessary.

Do not commit this correction until those checks pass. If Gradle reports an
error, copy the complete error text.

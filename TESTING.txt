IRON MILE — MILESTONE 10.2.0.2 CONTACT STABILITY

Install from the Iron Mile project folder while on authoritative-networking:

  ditto "$HOME/Downloads/ironmile-milestone10-2-0-2-update" .
  ./gradlew clean runClient

Do not commit until the following tests pass.

NORMAL MOVEMENT SMOOTHNESS
1. Drive in a straight line at low, medium, and high speed.
2. Make several gentle and sharp turns at each speed.
3. Drive across flat slabs and uneven road sections.
4. Check the car body, rider, camera, wheels, steering, and suspension for
   shaking or repeated small snaps.
5. Compare the movement with 10.2.0.1 and report whether the two-tick buffer is
   smooth enough or still visibly jittery.

WALL CONTACT HOLD
6. Approach a full-block wall slowly and confirm the visible bumper reaches it.
7. Keep holding W against the wall for at least ten seconds.
8. Confirm the car remains still instead of pulsing, shaking, or repeatedly
   rebuilding speed.
9. Release W, then press S and confirm reverse works immediately.
10. Repeat the same test rear-first while holding S, then escape with W.
11. Repeat with the front-left, front-right, rear-left, and rear-right corners.
12. Remove the wall or move away from it, then confirm normal acceleration
    returns and no old bumper contact remains stuck.

CONTACT ALIGNMENT
13. Repeat front and rear approaches at medium and high speed.
14. Note the approximate visible gap at the moment the HUD reaches zero.
15. Confirm neither bumper or corner visibly enters the wall.
16. Test a single-block pillar and a wall approached at a shallow angle.

AUTHORITY REGRESSION
17. Dismount while moving with W, S, W+A, W+D, S+A, and S+D.
18. Watch the car for at least 20 seconds after each dismount.
19. Confirm the delayed teleportation remains gone.

FEATURE REGRESSION
20. Confirm slabs remain climbable.
21. Confirm HUD speed, gear, RPM, surface, grip, and condition update.
22. Confirm engine audio, steering wheels, suspension, headlights, brake lamps,
    reversing lamps, and LambDynamicLights still work.
23. Save and reopen the world and confirm the car remains where it was parked.

Report separately:
- whether ordinary driving is now smooth;
- whether holding throttle against a wall is stable;
- the visible bumper gap at low, medium, and high speed;
- whether reversing away clears contact immediately;
- whether the dismount teleportation remains gone.

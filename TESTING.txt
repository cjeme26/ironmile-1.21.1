IRON MILE — MILESTONE 10.1.1 COLLISION AND SYNCHRONIZATION

Install from the Iron Mile project folder:

  ditto "$HOME/Downloads/milestone10-1-1-update" .
  ./gradlew clean runClient

Do not commit until the following tests pass.

BUMPER COLLISION
1. Spawn a fresh car facing a full-block wall.
2. Drive slowly into it. The visible front bumper should stop near the wall;
   the bonnet should no longer pass deeply through it.
3. Reverse into the wall. The visible rear bumper should also stop near it.
4. Approach the wall at a diagonal and with either bumper corner.
5. Confirm the car does not stop excessively far away from the wall.

SLABS
6. Drive slowly toward a slab. The front wheels should reach it before the car
   climbs, and the bonnet should not sink into it.
7. Stop with only the front wheels raised and inspect the visible wheel contact.
8. Cross isolated slabs and the earlier 2–6-block slab/gap arrangements.
9. Repeat at approximately 40, 60 and 90 km/h.

POSITION SYNCHRONIZATION
10. Drive at least 50 blocks, stop, wait two seconds, then dismount.
11. Repeat but dismount immediately while the car is still rolling slowly.
12. Drive, stop and remount several times.
13. Watch for any jump backward, jump forward, disappearance, or snap back.
14. Save and quit after driving, reopen the world and verify the saved location.

REGRESSION
15. Check steering, wheel animation, suspension, grip, transmission and audio.
16. Check all emissive light combinations and dynamic illumination.
17. Confirm door-side dismounting still works.

If teleporting remains, note whether it happens while mounted, exactly when
pressing Shift, a moment after dismounting, or after saving/reloading. A short
screen recording would also make the remaining trigger much easier to isolate.

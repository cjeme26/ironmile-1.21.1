IRON MILE — MILESTONE 9

Install from the root of your ironmile-1.21.1 project:

  ditto "$HOME/Downloads/milestone9-update" .
  ./gradlew clean runClient

Use third-person view and test in daylight first:

1. Enter the car and press H. The two FRONT lamps and HUD should switch On.
2. Press H again. The front lamps and HUD should switch Off.
3. Turn headlights on, leave/re-enter the world, and confirm they remain on.
4. Drive forward, then hold S to brake. Both red REAR lamps should brighten.
5. Release S after stopping. Brake lamps should return to dark red.
6. Reverse. The two small white REAR lamps should brighten.
7. Drive and brake in reverse with W; check both lamp types behave sensibly.
8. Confirm all lamps stay attached through pitch, roll, slabs, and turns.
9. Check Controls -> Key Binds -> Iron Mile -> Toggle Headlights.
10. Confirm engine audio and all driving behaviour remain unchanged.

The headlights do not illuminate terrain yet. Report if front/rear are swapped,
if H does nothing, if a lamp stays active incorrectly, or if placement is poor.

Suggested commit message:

  Add synchronized visual vehicle lights

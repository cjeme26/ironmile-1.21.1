IRON MILE — MILESTONE 10.1.3 DISMOUNT INPUT RELEASE

Install from the Iron Mile project folder:

  ditto "$HOME/Downloads/ironmile-milestone10-1-3-update" .
  ./gradlew clean runClient

Do not commit until the following tests pass.

PRIMARY TELEPORT TEST
1. Spawn a fresh car and drive for at least 20 seconds.
2. While the car is moving slowly, hold A and press Shift without releasing A.
3. Stand still and watch the car for at least 20 seconds.
4. Repeat step 2 with D instead of A.
5. Repeat while stopped completely but still holding A or D during dismount.
6. Repeat the four tests at least five times each.
7. Confirm the car never jumps backward, forward, away, or away and back.

CONTROL-RELEASE TEST
8. Dismount while holding W, S, A, and D separately.
9. Remount after each test and confirm no old key remains active.
10. Confirm the front wheels return to their neutral visual angle when empty.
11. Confirm brake or reverse lamps do not remain active after leaving the car.

NORMAL DISMOUNT TEST
12. Drive at least 50 blocks, stop, wait two seconds, then dismount.
13. Dismount immediately while rolling slowly without holding a steering key.
14. Mount, drive, and dismount ten times in succession.
15. Save and reopen the world and verify the car remains where it was left.

REGRESSION
16. Confirm W/A/S/D still drive and steer normally.
17. Test front, rear, and diagonal wall collisions.
18. Confirm slabs remain climbable.
19. Check wheel animation, suspension, engine audio, HUD, and door dismounting.
20. Check headlights, brake lights, reversing lights, and LambDynamicLights.

If teleporting remains, record whether it occurs only while A/D is held, the
approximate delay in seconds, whether the car is still moving, and whether it
returns to the exact point where the player first mounted or a more recent point.

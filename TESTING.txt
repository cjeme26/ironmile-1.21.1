IRON MILE — MILESTONE 8.3

Install from the root of your ironmile-1.21.1 project:

  ditto "$HOME/Downloads/milestone8-3-update" .
  ./gradlew clean runClient

1. Enter a stationary car and confirm idle is immediately audible as a rumble.
2. Let it idle for 15-20 seconds and listen for pulsing or flutter.
3. Accelerate and decelerate repeatedly through 10-100 km/h.
4. Listen carefully during each Gear: N shift moment.
5. Compare first- and third-person sound while riding.
6. Confirm throttle, reverse, distance, and dismount fades still work.

Please report idle and driving separately. For each, note volume, pitch, rumble,
and whether any broken-microphone/compressed-wind artifact remains.

  Refine idle and RPM audio transitions

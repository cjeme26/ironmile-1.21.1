IRON MILE — MILESTONE 6.5

Install from the root of your ironmile-1.21.1 project:

  ditto "$HOME/Downloads/milestone6-5-update" .
  ./gradlew clean runClient

Use a fresh car on flat stone in clear weather. Test these separately:

1. Accelerate forward to 10 km/h, release W, and note how quickly it stops.
2. Repeat from 20 km/h and 50 km/h. The 50 km/h test should coast longer.
3. Reverse to about 10-15 km/h, release S, and confirm it settles promptly.
4. Confirm the car reaches a true standstill instead of crawling indefinitely.
5. Confirm W and S pedal braking still work as before.
6. Confirm 0-100 km/h remains about 9 seconds on flat stone.

If the stopping behavior needs another adjustment, report the starting speed
and approximate number of seconds until the car completely stops.

Suggested commit message:

  Improve low-speed automatic stopping

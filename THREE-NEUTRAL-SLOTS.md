# Three neutral alignment slots

Neutral now has three internal positions while still appearing as one H-pattern bar:

- Left neutral: aligned with 1 / 2
- Center neutral: aligned with 3 / 4
- Right neutral: aligned with 5 / 6

Why:
- Moving from 3rd to 5th no longer requires precisely placing the knob under the 5th-gear rail.
- Horizontal mouse movement on neutral selects the adjacent neutral slot.
- A clearly vertical gesture enters the upper/lower gear for the current slot.
- Small diagonal mouse wobble is still treated as horizontal neutral movement.
- Inside a gear rail, horizontal wobble is ignored until the stick returns to neutral.
- Reverse/1st left-click toggle remains unchanged.

# Threshold H-track shifter

- The whole horizontal middle bar is neutral.
- Near neutral, horizontal movement is allowed.
- Vertical movement is allowed only near the 1/2, 3/4, or 5/6 columns.
- Away from neutral, horizontal movement is locked until the knob returns to the middle bar.
- Fast mouse input is split into small legal steps.
- Reverse remains the latched push-down upper-left gate.
